What's Happening at The Blind Walleye

Weekly Events

Weekly Bingo

Home of the Original Bingo Night in Town!

Join us every Wednesday from 5:30-7:30pm PM for a thrilling bingo extravaganza! Enjoy traditional bingo rounds at just $2/card, and amp up the stakes with our blackout round at $5/card.

Bring your friends and family for a chance to win fantastic prizes and create unforgettable memories! See you there!

April 13, 2024

Lucky King of Diamonds

Buy your tickets for $1 each to enter the pot, and if Lady Luck smiles your way and your number is called, you'll get the chance to pick a card from our securely locked display box. But here's where it gets even more thrilling – if the card you choose happens to be the illustrious King of Diamonds, you're walking away with the jackpot! Drawing happens at 7pm!

February 11, 2025

Trivia Night

Mark your calendars: Second & Fourth Tuesday of every month at 6pm

How it works:

*Form a team of 1-5 people (solo players, we got you too!).

*Put your knowledge to the test with some wild and wacky trivia questions!

*The top 3 teams with the highest scores will walk away with awesome prizes!

November 15, 2025

Puzzle Tournament

Think you’ve got the puzzle-solving skills to win? Teams of 4 will have 1 1/2 hours to put together a 500-piece puzzle. Prizes await the winning team! Plus, enjoy food & drink specials while you work your way to victory! $20 per team, sign up with bartender.

November 29, 2025

Singles Pool Tournament

🎱 Singles Pool Tournament at The Blind Walleye!

📅 Date: November 29
🕑 Time: 2:00 PM
💵 Entry Fee: $20 — cash prizes for the winners! Sign up day of the tournament.

🍺 Drink Special: $2.50 Busch Light cans

Grab your cue, show off your skills, and join us for a fun afternoon of competition, drinks, and good vibes!
