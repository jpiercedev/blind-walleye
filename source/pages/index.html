About the Owners

Christine & Fred

We are excited and thankful to be the new owners of this bar & grill. It was a hard decision to change the name given the rich history of The Shoreline Inn Bar & Grill. But we made the decision to change the name to one that has special meaning to our family: The Blind Walleye.

Fred and I both grew up in very large families with strong values such as hard work, loyalty, truth, and honor, which will be reflected in the way we run this establishment. We have two sons, Ethan 22 and Cole 18, who you will see helping us build this business and supporting the community.

Given Fred’s service as a former Marine and having a couple brothers of my own in the Army we plan on hosting several functions throughout the year to raise awareness and funds to support local Veterans, First Responders and their families. We want to honor them for risking their lives to keep all of us safe.

With the help of our family, friends, our team members and all of you, we can’t wait to see our dream come to reality. New name, new memories and good times for all of us.

Come visit us and enjoy our Midwest charm, beautiful views, drinks and great food!!

All our best,
Christine & Fred Chambers
